Zhang et al. compared three formulations: 4 m ZnOTf₂ (SE), 4 m ZnOTf₂ + 1 m ZnOAc₂ (DE), and 2.8 m ZnOTf₂ + 0.7 m ZnOAc₂ with 30 wt% DMF relative to water mass (HDE), where m denotes molality. FTIR deconvolution of the O–H stretching region at 3200–3600 cm⁻¹ indicated a lower strongly hydrogen-bonded contribution in DE and HDE than in SE, with the smallest contribution in HDE. This redistribution supports modification of the water hydrogen-bond environment, although FTIR does not directly measure water activity or hydrogen-evolution kinetics. The accompanying changes in salt concentration also preclude attributing the HDE response solely to DMF.

Raman spectra provided complementary evidence for anion coordination. Fitting of the OTf⁻ SO₃ stretching band assigned approximately 36.9% to contact ion pairs (CIPs) in SE; analysis of the acetate band in DE yielded approximately 64.5% CIPs and 12.4% aggregates (AGGs). Because these fractions derive from different anions and bands, their difference cannot quantify an increase in the overall CIP population. More complex acetate features in HDE prevented reliable extraction of corresponding fractions. Zn K-edge XANES edge shifts and R-space EXAFS changes further support rearrangement of the local Zn environment in the DMF-containing formulation, without resolving a unique coordination geometry.

The reported molecular dynamics (MD) results help interpret these spectral changes. The simulated solvent-separated ion-pair fraction decreased from 72.57% in SE to 50.41% in DE and 41.82% in HDE, while the AGG fraction fell from 33.07% in DE to 25.45% in HDE. The modeled change therefore involves redistribution among associated species. DMF entered the simulated Zn²⁺ solvation shell with an average coordination number of 0.19. These model statistics are distinct from Raman peak fractions. Together, the observations and simulations support coupled changes in water interactions and Zn solvation as a plausible contribution to improved plating/stripping reversibility, without establishing a unique causal pathway. The reversibility gains remain conditional, as the strategy was reported to be unsuitable when very high current density and very high areal capacity were combined.
