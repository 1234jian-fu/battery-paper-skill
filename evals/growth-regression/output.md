## A：跨方法表征

Operando XRD detected phase P during mid-discharge at 80 mA g−1 with an electrode loading of 4 mg cm−2. In a separate cell tested at 400 mA g−1 with a loading of 2 mg cm−2, operando microscopy showed bright regions expanding during discharge. Subsequent ex situ elemental mapping showed product-enriched regions. Brightness was not calibrated against chemical standards, and the elemental maps were not registered point by point with the operando images. These results are consistent with staged product formation but do not establish phase P nucleation at individual bright regions or exclude competing reaction pathways.

**关键调整：** 分开交代两颗电池及各自电流、载量，删除“同一电池同步观测”。将相P出现、亮区扩大和离线产物富集分别按实际测量身份陈述；亮度未经化学标准品校准、离线图与原位图未逐点配准，因此不把亮点直接指定为相P成核位置。保留“支持分阶段形成解释”，撤回唯一反应路径结论。

## B：性能与摘要口径

The laboratory full cell delivered a specific energy of 92 Wh kg−1, normalized to the combined mass of the positive- and negative-electrode active materials. A model of a twelve-layer pouch-cell design, including inactive-component masses, estimated a specific energy of 74 Wh kg−1. The pouch-cell value is a design projection; the modeled cell has not been fabricated.

**英文摘要句：** A laboratory full cell delivered 92 Wh kg−1 based on the combined active-material mass of both electrodes, while a twelve-layer pouch-cell design model including inactive-component masses projected 74 Wh kg−1.

**关键调整：** 将92 Wh kg−1明确为实验室全电池实测结果，并保留双电极活性物质质量分母；将74 Wh kg−1明确为计入非活性部件的十二层软包设计估算，不暗示已制备或测试软包。摘要沿用相同的数值身份与质量口径，删除缺乏同条件比较和商业化证据的纪录、实用化及商业化判断。

## C：模型、段落与篇级主线

To examine the formation of porous deposits, we modeled the diffusion and deposition of solution intermediates. The simulated porous morphology qualitatively resembled the microscopy images. The model also predicted the direction of the porosity change with concentration, which agreed with the comparison between the two tested concentrations. Together, these results support a qualitative diffusion–deposition explanation for porous growth in the system examined. Electron conduction was not included in the model, so its possible role in limiting growth remains unresolved. Quantitative morphological agreement and applicability across systems have yet to be established.

**关键调整：** 按“待解释现象—模型范围—形貌一致性—浓度预测及其对照—有限解释”组织段落。模型未纳入电子传导，不能据此排除电子输运限制；形貌相似和两种浓度的方向一致支持当前体系中的定性解释，不能证明分子机制或普适设计原则。没有补写浓度升降或孔隙率增减的具体方向。

1. 引言保留“多孔沉积如何形成”的问题，将拟回答范围限定为溶液中间体扩散与沉积是否能够解释所见形貌及浓度响应。
